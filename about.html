02 MY STORY

Book marketing should begin with listening.

I believe in work that feels honest, respectful and grounded in understanding the author, not simply promoting a title.

• WHO I AM

I am Hamied A. Edrees, a book marketer who supports authors and their books with care and attention.

I believe every book has its own audience. Meaningful marketing starts by understanding the work, the author and the readers they hope to reach. When those pieces come together, the path forward becomes much clearer.

My role is to help make that path easier to walk. That may mean shaping a launch plan, strengthening an author's online presence, creating promotional content or identifying practical ways to keep a book visible long after publication day.

I value clear communication, thoughtful planning and work that respects the author's voice. The aim is to make marketing feel more manageable, more focused and genuinely connected to real goals, rather than a never-ending list of tasks.

Behind every strategy I recommend is a simple belief. Authors deserve a partner who treats their book with the same care they put into writing it.
