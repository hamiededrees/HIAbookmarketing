01 TOOLS FOR YOUR NEXT STEP

Build a stronger foundation for your book.

Before you start posting on social media or reaching out to reviewers, it helps to get a few foundational pieces in place. These three areas will make everything else you do more focused, more effective and far less stressful.

02 PRACTICAL STARTING POINTS

Three areas that can make an immediate difference.

These are the areas I discuss most often with the authors I work with. Each one is manageable on its own, but together they create a much stronger foundation for your book's visibility.

01 ↗

A simple book launch planning checklist

A successful launch does not require a massive budget or a huge following. It requires preparation. Start by defining your audience, preparing your book information, planning your announcement and choosing a manageable promotional schedule. Even a modest, well-organised launch can create real momentum for your book.

02 ✦

Make your author profile clearer

Readers do not just connect with books. They connect with authors. Use a recognisable author name, a concise and warm biography, a clear description of the kind of stories you tell and consistent links to the places readers can find you and your work. A strong profile turns casual browsers into loyal followers.

Improve your author presence →
03 ◎

What makes a book description work?

Give readers a reason to care within the first sentence. Lead with an engaging hook, establish the premise clearly, communicate the tone and emotional promise of your story, and finish with a compelling invitation to read more. Your description should feel like the beginning of the reading experience, not a summary of it.

03 MORE STARTING POINTS

Keep building your author foundation.

Three more practical areas that often make a meaningful difference to a book's long-term visibility and an author's confidence in promoting their work.

04 ✦

Post with a purpose

You do not need to be everywhere. Choose two or three platforms your readers actually use and share a simple, authentic mix of story insights, creative process and personality. Readers want to feel connected to the person behind the book, not sold to by a marketing machine. Consistency matters more than frequency.

Plan your content strategy →
05 ↗

Approach reviewers with care

Reviews matter enormously, but the way you seek them matters just as much. Target reviewers who already cover your genre, keep your requests short and personal, respect their time and never pay for or pressure reviews. A genuine, thoughtful approach will always yield better results than a mass email.

Discuss review outreach →
06 ◎

Start a reader newsletter

A simple signup invite and a regular, friendly email gives you a direct line to your readers that no algorithm can take away. You do not need thousands of subscribers to make it worthwhile. Even a small, engaged list of readers who genuinely want to hear from you is one of the most valuable assets an author can build.
