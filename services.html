Tell me about your book, your timeline and what you would like to achieve. I reply personally to every message, usually within two working days. There is no automated response, no generic reply, just a genuine conversation about your book and your goals.

02 ↗

Together, we identify the most useful priorities for your book. Then I confirm the activities, the timeline and the investment in writing before any work begins. You will always know exactly what you are getting and what it will cost, with no hidden fees or surprises.

Agreed activities are delivered and shared with you regularly, from content and campaigns to plans and outreach. You will receive clear updates throughout the process so you always feel informed and involved in the direction of your marketing.
